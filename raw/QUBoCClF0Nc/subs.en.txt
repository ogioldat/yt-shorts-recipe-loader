welcome to 30 minute meals where I show
you recipes that take less than 30
minutes to make today we're making my
favorite Korean noodle dish jaang man
you can make it at home in no time and
it'll taste just as good as it looks in
those kramas start by frying black bean
paste in oil to get rid of that bitter
taste then chop onions and zucchini and
finally minced garlic and ginger slice
pork belly into chunks and sauté it
until it releases its fat and becomes
nice and brown add the onions garlic and
ginger and sauté until fragrant then add
the zucchini cook for a couple of
minutes before adding the cooked black
be paste and season with oyster sauce
gochugaru if you like it spicy sugar
black pepper and of course MSG pour in
some dashi broth and bring it to a
simmer before thickening with a
cornstarch lurry finish it with a little
sesame oil and now you could boil your
noodles for 1 minute less than package
instructions now plate it up with some
julan cucumber and serve it with pickled
radish and that's it enjoy
