Welcome to my Simply Tasty series where
I share you simple and satisfying dishes
to make at home. And today, we're making
Korean ground beef. First, lube up your
pan and add in some minced garlic and
cook until fragrant before adding in
your ground beef and breaking it apart
and letting it sit for a couple of
minutes. After your beef has mostly
browned, add gochujang, dark soy sauce,
light soy sauce, sesame oil, and sugar
and continue to cook until your sauce is
thickened around your beef before
removing from the heat. Finally, place
your beef over a fresh bowl of rice. You
can optionally garnish with sesame
seeds, scallions, and a fried egg, but
it's your food. You eat it how you like.
