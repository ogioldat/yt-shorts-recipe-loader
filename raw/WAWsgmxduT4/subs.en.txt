I'm making dishes that you'd actually
cook. Looks so good. The laziest way to
make a kefta sandwich. No skewers, no
crazy techniques. I'm Persian, which
means I wake up in the mornings thinking
about kebab, but it takes time and
effort to make. This sheet pan kepta
efficiently knocks out that craving. I
add onion, parsley, garlic, and just a
few spices. I like to work that together
before I add in the meat for more even
flavor distribution. Traditionally,
you'd skewer and grill these, but if
you're lazy like me, form it into a
block and use a spatula to cut into 8 in
keftas. I throw in some quarter
tomatoes. Give everybody a good gug of
olive oil and roast. I love that this
method is hands-off because as the ketos
go, I have the time to work on some
baller condiments. Quick sumac onions
and a harissa tahini. Creamy, garlicky,
spicy. It's exactly what this sandwich
needs. When the kefts are cooked 90% of
the way, I finish them over a broil for
that caramelized outside. Soak those
juices up with your bread of choice and
build your perfect sandwich. I was so
excited here that I forgot the onions.
Optionally, toast your sandwich in a pan
to help it seal together, and you're
left with what is now one of my proudest
creations in this series.
