Welcome to my healthy Asian dinner
series where I show you the best high
protein dishes to make. And today we're
making a garlic chicken stir fry. First,
marinate some cubed picky boy thighs and
shaing wine, salt, and cornstarch for 15
minutes. While the chicken marinates,
finely mince some garlic. Cut your
onions into thick wedges and cut your
scallions into 1/2-in pieces, separating
the whites and green. For the sauce,
combine dark soy sauce, light soy sauce,
oyster sauce, chicken bullion, sugar,
and water, and mix thoroughly. Now, lube
up your pan and add your chicken and
brown for about 5 minutes before
removing. Then in the same pan, add in
your onions, garlic, and scallion whites
and cook for 2 minutes or until
softened. Next, add in your chicken as
well as your sauce and continue stirring
around until the sauce is thickened
before adding in your scallion greens
and cooking until wilted. Finally, place
your chicken over a bowl of rice or you
can eat it as is. It's your food. You
eat it how you like.
