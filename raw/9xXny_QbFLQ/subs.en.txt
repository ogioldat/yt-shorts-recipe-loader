Every weekend, I spend 1 hour doing one
simple thing so I never have to buy food
or go to the store during the week. When
I was in high school, I used to go to
McDonald's all the time with my friends.
And every time my friends and I would
get sausage, egg, and cheeses. And they
were great. But at the time, we looked
like this and nobody told us we could
much more easily look like this if we
just made them with healthier
ingredients at home. Because we never
realized we were getting the highest
[clears throat] calorie, lowest protein
items on the menu without even checking
and spending way too much money every
day. Each of these only costs $2.80. So
now, every weekend my friends and I
spend 1 hour prepping 14 of these maple
turkey sausage sandwiches you can use or
freeze for up to 6 months. And if we
ever do end up going to restaurants, we
always use MenuFit's app to find the
absolute healthiest thing on the menu so
we can always stay on track even in a
pinch. So either way, you've got no
excuse to become healthier.
