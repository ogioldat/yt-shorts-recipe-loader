Coming back with Japanese curry beef
bowls because in this economy, it's time
to learn how to cook at home. That's the
good stuff. Into your hot pan, add in a
squeeze of oil and the ground beef. Let
it get nice and crispy while seasoning
with salt and pepper. Then break it up
continuously until cooked through and
remove. In the same pan, add in diced
onion and chopped carrots. Once
softened, add in minced garlic, grated
ginger, and the most important
ingredient, a few spicy curry blocks.
Gently stir everything together, add the
beef back in, and pour in milk until
just combined. More if you like it a
little saucy. Finish it up with frozen
peas and tonkatsu sauce, ketchup, or
grated apple for sweetness. And that's a
recipe you need to know.
